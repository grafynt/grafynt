## The short version

- Get on the official WhatsApp Business API. Unofficial gateways get numbers banned, and a banned number takes your customer history with it.
- Automate the messages you answer most, not the ones that are most interesting. Usually that is hours, prices, availability and booking.
- Write the escalation rules before you write anything else. Decide what the agent must never answer alone.
- Feed it your real past conversations. That is what makes it sound like your business rather than a template.

## What to automate first

Read your last two hundred WhatsApp conversations and count how often each question appears. The list is almost always shorter than people expect: what do you charge, are you open now, do you have availability, where are you, and has my order shipped.

Those five categories are the whole first build. They are repetitive enough that a person answering them is expensive, and specific enough that an agent can answer them exactly right every time.

What should not be in the first build: anything involving a complaint, a refund, a medical or legal claim, or a price that gets negotiated. Those go to a person immediately. The agent's job there is to recognise the conversation and hand it over quickly, not to attempt it.

## Official API or an unofficial gateway

Most of the cheap options you will be offered are unofficial gateways, software that drives WhatsApp the way a person would, through an unregistered number.

| | Official Business API | Unofficial gateway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ban risk | Approved by Meta, low | Against the terms of service, numbers do get banned |
| Cost | Per conversation, billed by Meta | Cheaper up front, nothing recurring |
| Features | Templates, verified profile, multi-agent inbox | Whatever the tool reverse-engineered this month |
| If it breaks | Documented, supported | It stops working when WhatsApp changes something |

The gateway is genuinely cheaper and genuinely faster to start. It is the wrong trade for a business whose customer relationships live in that number, because the failure mode is losing the number and every conversation in it.

## When this is a bad idea

Do not do this yet if your message volume is low enough that one person handles it comfortably inside working hours. Automation earns its price by removing repeated work; if the repetition is not there, you are buying a solution to a problem you do not have.

Do not do this if your prices, services or hours are still changing weekly. The agent is only as accurate as what you tell it, and rebuilding it every fortnight costs more attention than answering the messages yourself would have.

Do not do this if the real problem is that nobody can find you. If AI answer engines and search do not name your business at all, more responsive WhatsApp will not fix an empty inbox. Start with the audit and find out which one you have.
