## The short version

- The work splits into four things, and only one of them should recur.
- One-off: the visibility audit, the competitor analysis, the website build.
- Recurring: Google Business Profile maintenance, because a profile genuinely decays.
- Published prices here: Rp 710.000 audit, Rp 1.250.000 competitor analysis, Rp 1.520.000 a month for profile work with a three-month minimum, websites from Rp 7.550.000.
- Buy the measurement first. It is the cheapest line on any price list and it is the one that tells you whether the others are worth buying.

## What you are actually paying for

**Measurement.** Running the questions your buyers ask through the assistants and ordinary search, recording who gets named, and checking what a crawler can actually retrieve from your pages. This is cheap, fast, and it is the only item that tells you which of the others you need. Buying anything before it is guessing with a budget attached.

**Analysis.** Taking apart the businesses that got named instead of you, query by query, and working out which of those positions are winnable in months, which would take years, and which are not worth contesting at all. It costs more than the audit because it is more work: three to five other businesses, not just your own position.

**Maintenance.** Keeping a Google Business Profile accurate and internally consistent with everything else written about you. This is the one that recurs, and it recurs for a real reason, covered below.

**Build.** A site structured so that each buying-intent question has a page that answers it outright and can be retrieved without JavaScript. A revamp keeps the URLs and structure that already rank; only a new build starts over.

## Why only one line should be monthly

This is the question worth asking any agency you talk to.

A Google Business Profile does not stay where you put it. Google reshuffles categories and adds attributes. Third parties, including competitors, can suggest edits to your listing and those edits can be accepted without you noticing. Hours drift around holidays. Reviews and questions arrive constantly, each one adding text in wording nobody has reconciled against your site. A one-off cleanup is measurably better for a while and then decays.

An audit does not decay, it dates. A finished website does not decay either; it needs adding to, which is a separate decision with a separate price.

So when a proposal puts everything on one monthly fee, the useful question is: **which specific thing are you doing again next month, and why does it need doing again?** If the honest answer is "reporting", that is a dashboard, not a service.

## What moves the numbers

| What | Why it moves |
| --- | --- |
| Number of services on your profile | Four services and one location is a different job from forty and six branches |
| Number of competitors analysed | The price here covers three to five; more is more reading |
| Whether your site copy exists | Writing it is the slow part of a build |
| Revamp or new build | A revamp inherits structure and URLs that already earn rankings |
| How many locations | Each one multiplies the consistency surface |
| How many languages | Two languages is close to two of everything |

Note what is not on that list: your industry, and how competitive you believe your market to be. Those change whether the work succeeds, not what it costs to do.

## What it costs

| Competitor analysis | Rp 1.250.000, covers 3-5 competitors | ~1 week |

| Workflow automations | Quoted per project, add-on only | 1-2 weeks |
| Website, new or revamped | from Rp 7.550.000 | 2-3 weeks |

Two bundles exist, priced below the sum of their parts: website plus audit at Rp 7.550.000, which is the audit included free with a build, and everything except automation at Rp 8.540.000 one-time plus the Rp 1.520.000 monthly profile fee. Only the one-time work is discounted in a bundle; the monthly fee is the same either way.

The website figure covers a revamp or company profile up to about five pages, and a custom multi-page build is quoted per project. The profile work is monthly because a Google Business Profile does not stay fixed: hours change, categories get reshuffled, and reviews and questions keep arriving. A one-off cleanup decays within months, so it is not sold that way.

The audit is where everything starts: whether search engines and AI assistants name your business for buying-intent questions, whether they describe it correctly, and which businesses get named instead of you, by name. Plus a retrieval and consistency test across your site, your Google Business Profile and your other listings. About three days.

## When none of this is worth buying

Do not buy if nobody is searching for what you sell. This work captures demand that already exists. If you are creating a category, you need demand generation, which is a different job and not this one.

Do not buy a retainer before a measurement. The order matters more than the vendor. A measurement is cheap and tells you which problem you have; a retainer bought first commits you to someone else's guess about it.

Do not buy profile maintenance if you do not serve customers in a place. The monthly line earns its keep on local intent. Without a geographic component, put the money into answering questions on your own pages instead.

Do not buy a rebuild as the first move. Most visibility problems are fixed without touching the design, and the expensive step is the one most often sold first.
