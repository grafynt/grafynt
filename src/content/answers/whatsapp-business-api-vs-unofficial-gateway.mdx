## The short version

- The official Business API is Meta's supported product: approved numbers, a verified profile, message templates and documentation.
- An unofficial gateway automates WhatsApp the way a person would, through an unregistered number. It works until Meta notices.
- The gateway's advantages are real: cheaper up front, no per-conversation charge, live in a day.
- The gateway's failure mode is losing the number and every conversation inside it, with no reliable appeal.
- If your customer relationships live in that number, the trade is not close. Use the official API.

## How the two actually work

The official WhatsApp Business API is a documented interface. Your number is registered with Meta through a provider, your business name is verified, and you send and receive messages through a supported endpoint. Meta charges per conversation, billed to your own account. Because the arrangement is sanctioned, nothing about it depends on Meta not noticing you.

An unofficial gateway works from the other direction. It reverse-engineers the WhatsApp client — usually by running a logged-in session on a server — and exposes it as an API. Nothing is registered and nothing is approved. This is why it costs almost nothing per message: no one is billing you, because no one agreed to serve you.

That difference explains everything else. Templates, verified profiles, multi-agent inboxes and support tickets exist on one side because there is a contract. On the other side there is a tool that keeps working until WhatsApp changes something.

## When a gateway is defensible

There are narrow cases, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

Testing an idea. If you are proving that customers will book over WhatsApp at all and the number is disposable, a gateway answers that question in a day for almost nothing. Treat the number as disposable in fact, not just in theory.

Internal use. A number only your own staff message — shift alerts, stock checks, internal approvals — carries no customer history and no reputational cost if it disappears.

Volumes so low that per-conversation billing is genuinely awkward to set up. Even then, weigh it against what the number is worth to you rather than against the monthly saving.

Outside those, the gateway is a cheaper version of the thing you cannot afford to lose.

## When neither is worth doing yet

Do not put automation on any WhatsApp number if your volume is comfortably handled by the people you already have inside working hours. The platform question only matters once there is repetition to remove.

Do not migrate to the official API this month if your business verification documents are not in order. Half-finished verification blocks the build and produces a bad fortnight. Get the paperwork ready first, then start.

Do not spend anything here if the inbox is quiet. A more reliable channel does not create demand. Start with the audit and find out whether AI answer engines name your business at all — that is a different problem with a different fix.
